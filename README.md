# Amazon Sales Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon sales data using **Power BI** to identify sales trends, product performance, customer order patterns, geographic performance, fulfilment performance, and B2B vs B2C sales distribution.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Track overall sales performance using key KPIs.
- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Identify high-performing product categories, styles, and SKUs.
- Understand order status and delivery performance.
- Analyze sales performance across states and cities.
- Compare Amazon and Merchant fulfilment.
- Analyze the distribution of B2B and B2C sales.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Executive Overview

- Total recorded sales reached **₹78.59M**.
- Delivered revenue was **₹18.65M**.
- The dataset contains approximately **120K unique orders**.
- The average order value was **₹652.86**.
- Sales and orders peaked in **April** and declined through **May and June**.
- **Set** was the strongest category by a large margin, followed by **kurta** and **Western Dress**.
- Most orders were in **Shipped** status, while **Shipped - Delivered** was the second-largest visible status.
- A noticeable number of orders were **Cancelled**, indicating an area for further investigation.

### Product Performance

- **Set** had the highest average value per unit.
- **Saree** and **Western Dress** also showed relatively high average values per unit.
- The top-performing style generated nearly **₹2.9M** in sales.
- The top SKU generated over **₹0.5M** in sales.
- **Set** and **kurta** dominated the quantity sold, making them the primary volume-driving categories.

### Geographic & Fulfilment Analysis

- **Maharashtra** generated the highest sales among the displayed states.
- **Bengaluru** was the top city by recorded sales, followed by **Hyderabad** and **Mumbai**.
- **Amazon fulfilment** generated higher sales than Merchant fulfilment.
- **B2C sales overwhelmingly dominated** the dataset, while B2B contributed only a small portion.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Investigate the decline in sales and orders after April to identify potential causes.
2. Focus inventory and product strategies on high-performing categories such as **Set** and **kurta**.
3. Further investigate the reasons behind cancelled orders and identify opportunities to reduce cancellations.
4. Analyze the top-performing states and cities to identify opportunities for targeted marketing and inventory planning.
5. Evaluate the potential to improve Merchant fulfilment performance.
6. Explore opportunities to grow the relatively small B2B sales segment.
